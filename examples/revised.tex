\section{Illustrative minimization problem}
The heuristic does not guarantee optimality.
Let $\OPT$ denote the optimal objective value of the minimization problem.
If $L$ is a valid lower bound and $U$ is the objective value of a feasible solution, then
\begin{equation}
L \le \OPT \le U.
\label{eq:bounds}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:bounds} bounds the optimum under these conditions.
